# Production audit — 16 November 2026 (Europe/Kyiv)

Scope: corporate Wallpanels-com/sales-control, Supabase jqzhvnswwvbsqwwhcewn and the existing Railway production service. No Telegram test, demo or preview messages were sent. CRM requests were read-only. Normal production notifications remained enabled.

## Findings and fixes

- Both company export loops and Telegram polling were active. Production SLA remains 60/90/120 minutes; follow-up delays remain 24/48/96 hours, advanced by actual manual follow-up sends.
- Email history uses thread IDs and `meta.email.messageIds`, while exports use individual email IDs. Eleven pending Email incidents could not pass the original history gate. The client now expands bounded email threads via the read-only Email endpoint and unwraps the live `emailMessage` response, preserving each email's actual time, source, direction and company/contact scope.
- Internal CRM comments and unsuccessful/scheduled outbound messages could count as human replies. They are now excluded. Historical internal comments are also excluded from AI context.
- Unavailable conversations retried and logged every minute. Verification now backs off 15/30/60 minutes, resets for a new inbound and prunes closed incidents from the in-memory retry map. One historical Instagram conversation and its contact return CRM 400/not-found; no alert is sent from unverifiable history.
- Failed follow-up rows could hold the front of the ten-item queue. Failures and no-delivery outcomes now defer 30 minutes; confirmed prior delivery timestamps recover the reminder state without resending.
- Company/staff caching and the shared Supabase quota circuit remain enabled. Database size at the initial check was 48 MB; there was no quota block. No messages, tables or notification history were deleted.
- Updated Fastify/Zod and transitive fast-uri with compatible fixes. Production npm audit reports zero known vulnerabilities after update.

## Historical review

During initial historical reconciliation, six old messages received a local missing-key fallback classification. The procedure was stopped; no open incidents or sent alerts referenced those six fallbacks. Those six temporary classification results were reset. The procedure was changed to correct only verified resolution metadata without importing ingestion or AI.
